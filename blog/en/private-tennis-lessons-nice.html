Tennis in Nice: residents and visitors

In Nice I mostly meet two kinds of players: people who live here and want to improve over time, and people on holiday who want to enjoy an hour of tennis. Many don't speak French, and having the lesson in English or Italian makes a real difference.

Nice is also a good starting point for the rest of the Riviera, from Monaco to Antibes and Cannes.

Areas covered around Nice

If you have a court, at a villa, a residence or a hotel, I come to you with the balls. If you don't, message me and I'll suggest a solution in Nice.

I travel across Nice and much of the French Riviera: Nice, Antibes, Cannes, Monaco, Cap Ferrat, Beaulieu-sur-Mer, Èze, Menton, Mougins, Biot, Valbonne.

Common mistakes when choosing a coach

  • Not checking qualifications. In France, teaching tennis for a fee requires a recognised qualification. Ask which one.
  • Not saying what you want. An hour to have fun and an hour to fix your serve are not prepared the same way.
  • Choosing a coach who doesn't speak your language. Technical corrections land much better when you really understand each other.
  • Waiting until the last day of your stay. If you want to work on something, start early so there's time to come back to it.

How a private lesson works

First session

We talk for a few minutes about your experience and what you want to work on, then I watch you play before correcting anything.

Ongoing sessions

Drills built around your level and, with every correction, a simple explanation. The aim is for you to understand what you're changing.

Logistics

I come to your court at the agreed time, with the balls. Everything is arranged on WhatsApp.

Quick guide for visitors

  • Message me on WhatsApp before or during your stay: I reply within 24 hours.
  • For a short stay, two or three sessions a week work well.
  • You can book a single session and add more later.
  • We set the times together, around your stay.

Guide for residents

Consistency makes the difference

To really improve, one lesson a week with the same coach works better than occasional sessions.

The whole family

Children, parents and grandparents can have their lessons on the same day, each at their own level.

Easy to organise

No registration or paperwork: one WhatsApp message is enough to set up the first lesson.
