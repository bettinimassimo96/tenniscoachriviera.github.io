About

Certified coach,
10 years of teaching

I'm Massimo Bettini, an FITP-certified tennis coach (2nd level), based in Nice. I hold a 2nd-level diploma from the Italian Tennis and Padel Federation and have been teaching children, adults and seniors for ten years, first in Italy, now on the French Riviera.

I also help organise an ATP Challenger tournament in Italy. On court, I aim for something simple: clear lessons, suited to your level, that make you want to come back and play.

FAQ

What you want to know

Do you come directly to me?
Yes. I come to your court, at a villa, a residence or a hotel, on the French Riviera.
I don't have a private court. Can I still take lessons?
Yes. I book the court and bring the rackets. Message me with where you are, your availability and the number of players.
Do you cover Monaco, Antibes and Valbonne?
Yes. I travel across much of the French Riviera: Nice, Monaco, Antibes, Cannes, Valbonne, Sophia Antipolis, Mougins, Biot, Cap Ferrat and nearby.
Can I book just one session?
Yes, you can book a single session. If you live locally, we can also plan regular lessons.
Do you also come to hotels and villas?
Yes. If your villa or hotel has a court, I come to you at the agreed time. If not, I book a court nearby.
Do you offer lessons in English or Italian?
Yes, in English, French or Italian.
