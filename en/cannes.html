Private tennis coach in Cannes

On your court or on a court I book for you · Français, English, Italiano

On holiday in Cannes and looking for a private tennis coach in Cannes? If your villa, rental or hotel has a court, I come to you for a one-hour lesson suited to your level.

The idea is simple: make the most of your stay by playing well, alone, as a pair or as a family, with a few tips that actually help. No court? I book a court in the area and bring rackets and balls.

On your court

Villa, holiday rental or hotel with a court: I come to you with the balls.

As a family

Parents and children can play one after the other or together, each at their own level.

A single session is fine

Only here for a few days? Book one lesson, then decide if you want more.

No court?

Many of my students don't have one either. I'll book a court in the area and bring rackets and balls. All you need to do is turn up and play.

Frequently asked questions

I'm renting a villa in Cannes for the holidays. Can you come?
Yes, if the villa has a court. Just send me the address and your availability.
I don't have a court. Is it still possible?
Yes. I book a court in the area and bring rackets and balls. Tell me where you are, your availability and the number of players.
Can we have a family lesson?
Yes. Parents and children can share the session, each at their own level.
I haven't played in years. Is that a problem?
No. We start gently, at your pace, so you enjoy playing again.
