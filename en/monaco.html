Frequently asked questions

Do you come to residences in Monaco?
Yes. If your residence or villa has a court, I come to you, in Monaco and the neighbouring towns.
I don't have a court. What do you suggest?
I book a court a few minutes from Monaco and bring the rackets. Tell me where you are, your availability and the number of players.
I want to fix one specific shot. Is one lesson enough?
One lesson is enough to understand where the problem comes from and leave with a clear direction. For the change to stick, a few sessions close together work better.
Are lessons available in English?
Yes, in English, French or Italian.
